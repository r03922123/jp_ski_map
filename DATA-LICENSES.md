**ODbL note.** The OpenStreetMap-derived files stay under ODbL, and any database built from them falls under its share-alike terms ([ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) §4.4); maps and other Produced Works made from them don't (§4.5.b). That doesn't change the licence of the code that reads them. `stop_evidence.json` states the notice in its `_doc` field; the other JSON files carry none of their own, so this file is the notice.

**Resorts, bus stops and stop evidence.** ODbL covers each of these three files as a whole, not only its OpenStreetMap rows ([ADR-0002](docs/adr/0002-data-files-odbl.md)). In `bus_stops.json` and `stop_evidence.json` one coordinate field mixes OpenStreetMap values with values from elsewhere. That falls outside the safe harbour of the OSMF's [Collective Database Guideline](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Licence/Community_Guidelines/Collective_Database_Guideline_Guideline), so share-alike can reach the whole file ([ODbL](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/) §4.4.a, §4.4.b). The coordinates in `resorts.json` are all from OpenStreetMap; it gets the same label by choice, so one rule covers all three files. From OpenStreetMap: 53 of the 100 stops (25 OSM points, 8 from the lodging snapshot, 7 via Photon, 13 resort access points), all 12 resort access points, and 21 of the 66 evidence entries.

Coordinates from another open source keep their own licence as well ([ADR-0004](docs/adr/0004-open-coordinate-sources.md)):

- **30 Overture Maps Places points:** the stops whose `coordSource` starts with `overture:`, and the same 30 entries in `stop_evidence.json`. From release 2026-10-21.0: `stop-hotel-ogiya`, `stop-meteor` and `stop-pals-inn-raicho` are Foursquare records, and the other 27 are Meta records (see "Overture Maps Places" below).

One group is named but not covered, and follow-up 1 stays open until it is settled. When checked on 2026-11-06, these 15 pins had no licence-clean point, so they wait on the owner's permission requests ([ADR-0004](docs/adr/0004-open-coordinate-sources.md) next step 3):

- **15 Google My Maps pins:** the stops whose `coordSource` starts with `operator My Maps pin`, and the same 15 entries in `stop_evidence.json`. Copied from the bus operator's Google My Maps; their licence is unresolved. In `stop_evidence.json` these pins sit under a key named `osm`, but they are not OpenStreetMap data: their `ref` starts with `operator My Maps pin`.

The GSI-geocoded group ended in [PR #22](https://github.com/r03922123/jp_ski_map/pull/22): its one stop, `stop-panorama-hotel`, moved to Overture. The other 2 stops have no coordinates yet (`coord` is `null`).

**Overture Maps Places.** `lodging_overture.json` and the 30 Overture stops come from release 2026-10-21.0 ([attribution](https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/)). Each record keeps its provider's licence. Meta and Microsoft records are under CDLA-Permissive-2.0, whose text is in [LICENSES/CDLA-Permissive-2.0.txt](LICENSES/CDLA-Permissive-2.0.txt) (from [cdla.dev](https://cdla.dev/permissive-2-0/)). Foursquare records are under Apache-2.0 ([LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt](LICENSES/Apache-2.0.txt)) with Foursquare's [NOTICE](LICENSES/Foursquare-NOTICE.txt), copied on 2026-11-06 from <https://fsq-os-places-us-east-1.s3.amazonaws.com/NOTICE.txt> (© 2025). The [NOTICE page](https://opensource.foursquare.com/places-notice-txt/) that Overture's attribution links has the same terms under © 2026, and Overture's own notice below says 2024. They are the 3 stops above and 62 of the 755 records in `lodging_overture.json`; `lodging_overture.meta.json` lists every provider with its count. For them Overture states: "Copyright 2024 Foursquare Labs, Inc. All rights reserved. Available under Apache 2.0. Foursquare data was transformed to the Overture schema. Changed: 2026-03-18." **This project modified these records** (Apache-2.0 §4(b)): it reshaped them into its own schema, in `lodging_overture.json` with names split into English and Japanese, a kind derived from the category and name, and the centre of the record's bounding box as its point, and it rounded the 3 stop points to 5 decimal places.

**AllThePlaces records.** Two places in `lodging_overture.json` come from AllThePlaces under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): `overture:b5e49df0-4034-4650-b4cd-caf604c85126` (Holiday Inn Resort Shinano-Omachi Kuroyon) and `overture:578398a7-298f-46cf-a303-d1455b802014` (Hakuba no sato Schoendorf Youth Hostel). The waiver covers AllThePlaces' compilation, but its points are scraped from brands' own sites, so where they came from remains a risk. [ADR-0004](docs/adr/0004-open-coordinate-sources.md) keeps this source out of the bus stops; these two lodging records stay, named here.

**Test files.** These test files hold copies of excepted coordinates, exact or rounded (all within 6 m of the original). They were found on 2026-11-06 by comparing every tracked file with every My Maps or GSI coordinate that any version of `bus_stops.json` or `stop_evidence.json` has held. The copies fall under the same exceptions as the stops they copy, and the files are left as they are:

- `apps/web/e2e/fixtures/bus-options-2026-03-20.json`, a captured bus-options API response that the end-to-end tests serve in place of the live API: the old pins of `stop-snowlines-lodge`, `stop-3-parking`, `stop-cook` and `stop-echoland-spicy`, which [PR #17](https://github.com/r03922123/jp_ski_map/pull/17), [PR #22](https://github.com/r03922123/jp_ski_map/pull/22) and [PR #23](https://github.com/r03922123/jp_ski_map/pull/23) replaced in `bus_stops.json`. Regenerating it needs the running API.
- `apps/web/e2e/pilot.spec.ts`: `stop-echoland-spicy`'s old My Maps pin (before PR #23), used as a test GPS position.
- `services/api/tests/fixtures/mymaps/sample.kml`, a sample My Maps KML export for the importer tests: its Kanemaru placemark is `stop-kanemaru`'s pin. Its other 3 placemarks are in no version of the data files, so their source can't be checked here; they count as My Maps pins too.
- `services/api/tests/bus/test_import_mymaps.py`: the first of those 3 placemarks, and points within 5 m of the old My Maps pins of `stop-la-vigne-hakuba-north-parking` (before PR #23) and `stop-alpine-chalets` (before PR #17).
- `services/api/tests/routing/test_alternates.py`: `stop-spicy-rental-wadano`'s My Maps pin.
- `services/api/tests/places/booking_eval.json`: `stop-courtyard-by-marriott-hakuba`'s My Maps pin, rounded (the "operator's pickup pin").
- `services/api/tests/bus/test_locate_stops.py`: the old GSI points of `stop-panorama-hotel` (before PR #22) and `stop-cultured` (before PR #17), rounded. Like the old pins above, they stay named as copies of an exception: GSI's address search answers with CSIS Simple Geocoding ([gsimaps#29](https://github.com/gsi-cyberjapan/gsimaps/issues/29)), whose [terms](https://geocode.csis.u-tokyo.ac.jp/home/simple-geocoding/simple-geocoding-tems-of-use/) ask for a credit on maps and in apps (第4条(1), (4)).

**Timetables.** The operators' original PDFs and photos are kept out of the repository. Only the transcribed departure times are committed. They may be out of date or contain mistakes, so always check the official timetable before you travel.
