# UX review, 2026-11-03: four evaluators, laptop + phone

**Method.** A heuristic evaluation by four independent evaluators, each with one lens: first-time phone user, laptop trip planner, consistency auditor, resilience + accessibility. Three to five evaluators find most usability problems (Nielsen). They tested the production build (`next start`, the same build as the public site) with Playwright Chromium:
- laptop: 1440×900 and 1280×720, mouse and keyboard;
- phone: iPhone 15 emulation (393×659, touch), plus 360×740, 320×568 and 844×390 landscape.

Severity uses Nielsen's scale (4 = blocks a task, 3 = major, 2 = minor, 1 = cosmetic). The lead re-ran the top clusters independently: **V** = verified by the lead (script and/or REA evidence), not just reported.

Not covered: a real iOS keyboard, VoiceOver, real GPS sequences, gloved taps, an in-season bus day (live timetables start 2026-12-13, so testers used future dates), Android hardware. The real-iPhone checks are stage 5 of [rea-benchmark-workflow.md](rea-benchmark-workflow.md).

**What held up:**
- All 20 resort ETAs in the list match their routes.
- Swapping to an alternate updates every number.
- The URL round-trips sort, date, mode and resort.
- Laptop and phone show the same numbers.
- 500 errors are shown in place.
- Reduced motion, the title dialog focus trap and control names work.
- Japanese and very long search text work.
- No horizontal scroll at 320 px, landscape or 200 %.
- Sound is off by default.
- Sheet swipes, list scroll, and panning with the sheet open all work.

## Clusters, most severe first

Codes: M = phone, L = laptop, C = consistency, R = resilience/a11y. Type is **contradiction** (the app disagrees with itself or its spec) or **unfriendly** (it agrees with itself but works against the user).

### P3: polish (severity 1)

- C12: walk shows a "live traffic" disclaimer. C15: Drive's date isn't defaulted; Walk can't pick a bus stop. C16: Rider stats can't grow on a laptop.
- L17: 22 vs 23 min at a rounding boundary (1349 vs 1350 s). L18: right-click does nothing; after ✕ on To there's no box to type in.
- M17: line name vs "V" badge; game terms unexplained. M18: the search box's visible text ≠ its accessible name.
- R11: a short link that can't be read says "No matches". R15: invalid URL values are dropped silently; on slow 3G a shared link first says "set your starting point".
